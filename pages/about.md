Hey! My name is 周陈梁 ZHOU, Chenliang. I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Cambridge Open Reality and Visual AI Laboratory (<a href="https://core-lab.io" target="_blank">Core Lab</a>) in the <a href="https://www.cst.cam.ac.uk" target="_blank">Department of Computer Science and Technology</a> at the <a href="https://www.cam.ac.uk" target="_blank">University of Cambridge</a>, working with <a href="https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~aco41/" target="_blank">Cengiz Öztireli</a>.

My research sits at the intersection of **multimodal generative AI**, **visual foundation models**, and **3D vision**. I develop methods for controllable generation and editing of images, 3D shapes, and materials—spanning diffusion models, neural rendering, and physically grounded visual representations. More broadly, I am interested in building generative systems that are not only high-quality, but also structured, interpretable, and usable in real creative and industrial pipelines.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Cambridge, my MSc in Computer Science from the <a href="https://www.ox.ac.uk" target="_blank">University of Oxford</a>, and BSc (Hons) in Computer Science and BSc (Hons) in Mathematics from the <a href="https://www.ubc.ca" target="_blank">University of British Columbia</a>.

Outside research, I enjoy travelling, working out, and trying different kinds of sports.
